CSV Autopsy

What did the spreadsheet do to your file?

Open a CSV in Excel or Google Sheets, save it, and some of it quietly changes. Phone numbers lose their first 0, long order numbers turn into 2.02601E+15, dates swap day and month, and names like Peñaflor come back as PeÃ±aflor. This finds the damage, says what can be undone, and undoes it.

Drop a CSV file here, or choose one. It never leaves this page: the checks run in your browser.
